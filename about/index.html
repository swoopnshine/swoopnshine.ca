Cleaning that starts with the right questions.

Swoop N Shine provides residential and commercial cleaning across Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area. We work with homeowners, renters, hosts, property managers, businesses, event spaces, and contractors who need a clear plan and a dependable finish.

No two properties have exactly the same condition, materials, access, or deadline. That is why a useful quote begins with details: the type and size of the property, occupancy, room count, photos, special surfaces, parking or building rules, and the result that matters most.

What we value

Clear scope

We separate included tasks, optional add-ons, exclusions, and site requirements so expectations are practical before the appointment.

Material-aware methods

Hardwood, vinyl, epoxy, stone, stainless steel, glass, rubber flooring, and newly finished surfaces do not all accept the same products or tools. Labels and manufacturer guidance come first.

Respect for the property

Access instructions, pets, alarms, delicate belongings, confidential work areas, and client restrictions are confirmed and followed.

Honest boundaries

General cleaning does not include repairs, restoration, hazardous-material work, regulated waste, mould remediation, pest control, or unsafe high-access tasks unless an appropriately qualified service is separately arranged.

Where we work

Our service coverage includes Toronto, Brampton, Mississauga, Vaughan, Markham, Richmond Hill, Oakville, Burlington, Milton, and other GTA communities. Availability is confirmed using the exact address, requested service, date, travel, and access.
