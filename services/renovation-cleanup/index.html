A clear renovation cleanup plan for your space.

Even a contained renovation can spread fine dust through nearby rooms. We confirm the work zone, adjacent spaces, material residues, new finishes, ventilation status, and whether trades are completely finished before the final clean.

This service is designed for homeowners, contractors, designers, and property managers. The first step is a straightforward scope: what must be cleaned, what is optional, what condition the space is in, and when it must be ready. That information protects the booking from vague expectations and allows the right amount of time.

What can be included

The final checklist is written for your property. Typical tasks include:

  • Fine-dust vacuuming
  • Reachable surface wipe-down
  • New cabinet and fixture cleaning
  • Baseboard and trim detail
  • Floor cleaning suitable for new finishes
  • Adjacent-area touch-up when included
Important scope note

Heavy grease, excessive buildup, pest waste, mould, hazardous materials, high-level work, permanent staining, repairs, and specialized restoration are not assumed to be part of a general cleaning quote. Tell us about these conditions in advance so we can set safe expectations or recommend the appropriate specialist.

How we prepare an accurate quote

We may ask for the service address, approximate square footage, room and washroom count, occupancy, stairs, parking, building access, photos, and your preferred completion time. For commercial and post-construction properties, a walkthrough can be the fastest way to confirm floor materials, glass, dust level, equipment restrictions, and site rules.

A quote is most useful when it lists the included tasks and any options separately. If the space is substantially different on arrival, we discuss priorities and any price change before extra work begins.

Before the cleaning appointment

  1. Provide entry, parking, concierge, elevator, key, or alarm instructions.
  2. Remove personal clutter or identify anything that should not be moved.
  3. Secure pets and disclose sensitivities, delicate surfaces, or product restrictions.
  4. Confirm the priority rooms, deadline, and add-ons in writing.

Questions

Renovation Cleanup FAQs

These answers explain how scope, pricing, supplies, and Toronto/GTA coverage are handled.

What is included in renovation cleanup?

The confirmed checklist is based on the property, condition, timing, and priorities. Common inclusions are fine-dust vacuuming, reachable surface wipe-down, new cabinet and fixture cleaning, baseboard and trim detail. Any appliance interiors, windows, specialty treatment, or unusual access should be identified before the quote.

Do you bring supplies for renovation cleanup?

Yes. Swoop N Shine brings standard cleaning supplies and equipment for the agreed scope. Please tell us about delicate materials, product restrictions, allergies, or on-site requirements before the appointment.

How is renovation cleanup priced?

Pricing depends on property size, rooms, current condition, access, requested tasks, add-ons, and the crew time required. Photos or a walkthrough may be requested for larger, commercial, or heavily soiled spaces.

Which areas do you serve?

Swoop N Shine serves Toronto and many GTA communities. Availability depends on the exact address, scope, travel, and requested date, so the service location is confirmed before booking.
